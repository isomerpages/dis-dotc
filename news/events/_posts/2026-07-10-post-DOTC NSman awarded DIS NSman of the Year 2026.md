<p>Huge shoutout to CPT (NS) Liang Yuanruo, our DIS NSman of the Year 2026!
We’re so proud to have him as part of the DOTC family.</p>
<p>Some of you may remember him leading the charge at DOTC’s BuildTogether
Hackathon, where his team built a fully functional SpaceScanner in just
two weeks.</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">

<p>On top of that, he’s been quietly mentoring DIS software engineers, sharing
his insights with leadership and raising the engineering bar across the
board. He was also instrumental in helping to shape the growth and development
of professionals in the C4X (Digital) and DCX (Digital) vocations.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Thanks for everything you do, Yuanruo. By building things that matter,
by your laser focus on product and engineering excellence, you inspire
the rest of us along the way. Congratulations on your well-deserved award!</p>
